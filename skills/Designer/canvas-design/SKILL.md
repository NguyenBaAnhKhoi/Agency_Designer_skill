# Commercial Poster, Key Visual & 2D Canvas Design System

## 1. Overview & Core Philosophy
This skill provides an industrial-grade framework for designing **commercial posters, Key Visuals (KV), event artwork, exhibition banners, and 2D canvas collateral**.

It bridges the gap between AI image generation and professional graphic layout by strictly enforcing **Separation of Concerns**:
- **Layer 1: Background & Hero Visual** — Atmospheric imagery, 3D abstract shapes, or clean photography generated with Midjourney v6.1 / Flux / Venice AI with generous negative space.
- **Layer 2: Spatial Grid & Composition** — Strict 12-column, 3x3 rule-of-thirds, or modular grid layout.
- **Layer 3: Typography & Vector Accents** — Hierarchical headlines, subheads, metadata badges, QR codes, and sponsor lockups.

---

## 2. The 3-Zone Visual Hierarchy Rule
Every commercial poster and 2D artwork must guide the viewer's eye through 3 distinct distance zones:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [ZONE 1: Distance Recognition (30m - 50m / 3s Hook)]    │
│ Dominant Hero Visual or Massive Display Headline       │
│ Visual Weight: 50% - 60% of total canvas                │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ZONE 2: Mid-Range Communication (5m - 10m / 10s Read)]│
│ Core Message: Event Title, Date, Venue, Value Prop     │
│ Clean contrast, tight tracking, clear diacritics       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ZONE 3: Close Inspection (1m - 2m / Detail & Action)] │
│ Speaker/Artist Lineup, Ticket Tiers, QR Code, Sponsors │
│ Micro-typography, 8pt baseline grid, legal/credits     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Commercial Poster Archetypes

### Archetype A: Swiss Modernism (Grid Precision)
- **Aesthetic:** Mathematical order, structured asymmetry, high legibility.
- **Grid:** Strict 12-column or 6-column grid with visible or implied dividing lines.
- **Typography:** Bold Neo-Grotesque (Helvetica, Akzidenz, Inter, DM Sans) paired with neutral body copy.
- **Color:** Strict 2–3 color palette (e.g. Swiss Red `#FF0000`, Pure White `#FFFFFF`, Pitch Black `#111111`).
- **Best For:** Architecture exhibitions, corporate conferences, tech summits, cultural retrospectives.

### Archetype B: Neo-Brutalist & Street Energy
- **Aesthetic:** High-contrast, bold outlines, oversized typography, deliberate raw power.
- **Graphic Elements:** 3D extruded shapes, sticker pills, barcode stamps, monochrome halftone textures.
- **Typography:** Ultra-heavy grotesque or geometric display (Syne, Clash Display, Space Grotesk) with tight tracking (`letter-spacing: -0.04em`).
- **Color:** Acid accents (Neon Lime `#CCFF00`, Cyber Orange `#FF5500`) over deep charcoal `#121212`.
- **Best For:** Music festivals, street culture, hackathons, youth marketing campaigns.

### Archetype C: Editorial & Cultural Luxury
- **Aesthetic:** Refined elegance, vast negative space (35–45%), tactile depth.
- **Composition:** Hero portrait or high-end product photography anchored by subtle typographic whispering.
- **Typography:** High-contrast Serif display (Playfair, Bodoni, Cormorant Garamond) paired with airy Geometric Sans body.
- **Color:** Warm Terracotta, Forest & Moss, Midnight Navy with cream/off-white accents.
- **Best For:** Fashion galas, film premieres, luxury brand launches, art exhibitions.

### Archetype D: Dynamic Cyberpunk & Gradient Mesh
- **Aesthetic:** Volumetric lighting, glassmorphic card overlays, multi-stop organic gradients.
- **Visuals:** 3D procedural geometries or fluid meshes radiating from the center or diagonal axis.
- **Typography:** Clean futuristic sans, glowing accents, luminous pill badges.
- **Color:** Deep violet-indigo dark mode (`#0B0C10`) with cyan (`#00F0FF`) and magenta (`#FF007F`) rim lights.
- **Best For:** Esports championships, AI & web3 summits, EDM festivals.

## 4. Mandatory Graphic Design Rules (PPTX & Agency Standards)

To guarantee pristine quality when running on **GPT-6.1 Sol**:

1. **Color Dominance (The 60-30-10 Rule):**
   - **60% Dominant Tone:** Establishes canvas mood (e.g., deep charcoal `#0F172A` or pure white `#FFFFFF`).
   - **30% Secondary Structure:** Cards, visual blocks, secondary text (`#94A3B8`).
   - **10% Sharp Accent:** Directs attention to the primary CTA, date badge, or key headline.
   - *Never give all colors equal visual weight.*
2. **Spacing & Margin Enforcements:**
   - **Outer Canvas Margins:** Minimum `0.5"` (15mm – 25mm on A2/A1 prints, 60px – 100px on digital canvas). Never place text closer than 15mm to the canvas edge.
   - **Element Gaps:** Maintain consistent `24px / 32px / 48px` intervals between distinct information groups.
   - **Negative Space Budget:** Keep at least 25% to 40% of the canvas uncluttered so key elements breathe.
3. **Typography Anti-Patterns (Crucial for AI Quality):**
   - **NEVER use accent lines or underlines beneath titles** — these are dead giveaways of amateur AI output; use whitespace and scale contrast instead.
   - **NEVER add single-side decorative borders or arbitrary edge stripes** along cards.
   - **Left-align all body text and descriptions** — center-align ONLY main hero titles, badges, and sponsor bars.
   - **Never ship text that overflows or collides with imagery.**
4. **Vietnamese Diacritics & Character Safety:**
   - Full support for Vietnamese tone marks (`ả, ã, á, à, ạ`).
   - Prevent diacritic clipping on uppercase glyphs (`Đ`, `Ư`, `Ơ`, `Â`, `Ê`, `Ô`) by ensuring `line-height` is at least `1.25x` to `1.35x` the font size.

## 6. Execution Workflow: The Structured Blueprint (GPT-6.1 Sol Protocol)

When invoked, the Agent **MUST NOT** write vague philosophical poetry. It must execute the following 4-step protocol and produce a concrete **CANVAS BLUEPRINT**:

### Step 1: Design System Lookup via `ui-ux-pro-max`
Query the built-in design database to extract authentic colors, typography, and styling tokens:
```bash
python3 .agent/skills/Designer/ui-ux-pro-max/scripts/search.py "<industry/theme> poster" --domain style
python3 .agent/skills/Designer/ui-ux-pro-max/scripts/search.py "<industry/theme> palette" --domain color
python3 .agent/skills/Designer/ui-ux-pro-max/scripts/search.py "<vibe> pairing" --domain typography
```

### Step 2: Formulate the AI Background Prompt (Clean Visual Layer)
Construct a prompt using `Designer/ai-image-gen`:
`[Subject/Art Medium] + [Lighting & Depth] + [Composition with 40% Negative Space for Typography] + [--ar <ratio>]`
*Flag: Emphasize "clean space at top/center for text overlay, no letters, no watermark, no text".*

### Step 3: Emit the Structured Design Blueprint (`CANVAS_SPEC`)
Output the complete layout coordinate specification:

```markdown
### 📐 CANVAS DESIGN SPECIFICATION (BLUEPRINT)

**Project Name:** [Project Title]  
**Format & Size:** [e.g. A2 420x594mm / 4961x7016px @ 300 DPI]  
**Color Profile:** [CMYK / sRGB] — Bleed: [3mm / 35px]  

#### 🎨 Color System (60-30-10)
- **Dominant 60%:** `#HEX` (Base background & canvas atmosphere)
- **Secondary 30%:** `#HEX` (Structural card containers, secondary text, geometric shapes)
- **Accent 10%:** `#HEX` (High-visibility focal points, date stamps, badges)

#### 🔤 Typographic Hierarchy
| Role | Content Text | Typeface Family | Weight | Size / Leading | Alignment & Case | Tracking |
|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Hero Title (Zone 1)** | [TÊN SỰ KIỆN] | Display Sans / Serif | Black / 900 | 120pt / 1.1x | Center / Uppercase | -0.03em |
| **Eyebrow / Category** | [SPECIAL EXHIBITION] | Geometric Sans | Bold / 700 | 20pt / 1.2x | Center / Uppercase | +0.15em |
| **Core Details (Zone 2)** | [Date, Time, Venue] | Neutral Sans | SemiBold / 600 | 32pt / 1.3x | Left or Center | 0.00em |
| **Micro Copy (Zone 3)** | [Sponsors, Terms, QR] | Technical Mono/Sans | Regular / 400 | 12pt / 1.4x | Justified / Center | +0.02em |

#### 🗺️ Spatial Coordinate & Layer Stack
1. **Layer 0 (Background):** Base gradient / imported clean AI key visual.
2. **Layer 1 (Graphic Frame):** 12-column grid guides, safe margin boundary (20mm inset).
3. **Layer 2 (Header Zone - Y: 0% - 25%):** Eyebrow badge pill + Organization lockup.
4. **Layer 3 (Hero Zone - Y: 25% - 60%):** Focal graphic element + Primary Display Title.
5. **Layer 4 (Info Zone - Y: 60% - 82%):** Supporting schedule, speaker badges, ticket QR code.
6. **Layer 5 (Footer Zone - Y: 82% - 100%):** Sponsor logo grid (equal optical height 24mm) + Legal disclaimers.
```

- **If Adobe MCP is active:** Execute the JSX ExtendScript or BatchPlay descriptor.
- **If Standalone:** Output the production-ready SVG / HTML5 Canvas / PDF renderer script.
