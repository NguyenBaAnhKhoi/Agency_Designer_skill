# 🦊 Mascot & Character Model Sheet System

## 1. Core Mission & Philosophy
A campaign mascot or brand IP character acts as the emotional ambassador of the event. It bridges corporate brand messaging and audience engagement, appearing across social stickers, live-action merchandise, photobooths, and 3D stage visuals.

### Root Problem Solved:
Without a rigorous model sheet, mascots suffer from severe anatomical drifting across iterations (head proportions shift, limbs change lengths, colors vary between assets). This skill establishes an immutable **Character Turnaround Specification** and **Expression Matrix**.

```
└───────────────┴────────────────┴───────────────┴───────────────────────┘
```

### Anatomical & Proportion Standards:
- **Head-to-Body Proportion Scale**:
  - *Chibi / Cute Event Style*: 2.5 to 3 heads tall (High appeal, maximum merchandise viability).
  - *Heroic / Stylized Style*: 4 to 5 heads tall (Edgier music festival / cyber aesthetic).
- **Proportion Grid Guidelines**:
  - Horizontal reference lines must align across all 4 views: Top of Head, Eye Line, Chin/Collar, Belt/Waist, Knee, Sole of Foot.
- **Vector Silhouette Rule**: The character silhouette must remain recognizable in pure black fill at 32×32px.

## 3. The 6-Emotion Expression Matrix

Mascots must emote dynamically across social countdowns, ticket sales, and event announcements. Provide vector headshots for 6 core expressions:

1. **Hype / Euphoric (Phấn khích)**: Wide open sparkling eyes, huge smile, mouth open in celebration (Used for: Lineup drops, Ticket launch).
2. **Confident / Cool (Bản lĩnh)**: Slight smirk, arched eyebrow, wearing sunglasses or stage headset (Used for: Artist reveals, VIP announcements).
3. **Friendly / Welcoming (Thân thiện)**: Warm gentle smile, soft rounded eyes, head tilted slightly (Used for: Welcome gate, Event FAQ, Venue map).
4. **Curious / Anticipating (Tò mò)**: Wide inquisitive eyes, finger on chin, one eyebrow raised (Used for: Teasing phase, mystery clues).
5. **Shock / Urgency (Khẩn cấp)**: Wide cartoon pupils, open gasp, sweat drop or speed lines (Used for: Ticket sold-out warnings, Last chance).
6. **Party / Musical (Cháy hết mình)**: Eyes closed in musical bliss, wearing glowing lightstick wristbands, singing/cheering (Used for: Showday live feed).

## 4. Action Poses with Event & Product Props

Deliver at least 4 full-body master action poses:
1. **The Hero Prop Pose**: Mascot holding the featured launch product or holding an illuminated custom lightstick high in the air.
2. **The Ticket Master Pose**: Mascot presenting a golden VIP ticket with both hands toward the camera.
3. **The Stage Performer Pose**: Mascot wearing DJ headphones, fingers on a turntable or pointing forward to the crowd.
4. **The Selfie / Welcoming Pose**: Mascot leaning forward with a peace sign or waving to attendees at the entrance.

## 5. Color Specifications & 3D Shading Pipeline
- **Vector Palette**: Primary Fur/Skin Hex, Secondary Accent Hex, Eyes/Mouth Hex, Prop Accent Hex.
- **Stroke Standards**: Constant 3pt–4pt exterior contour outline; 1.5pt–2pt interior detail lines.
- **3D Production Notes (For Blender / C4D / Maya artists)**:
  - *Base Material*: Matte vinyl or soft silicone (Roughness: 0.35–0.45, Metallic: 0.0).
  - *Lighting Key*: Three-point studio lighting with high-contrast rim light matching the campaign secondary neon color.
